### On A1 — read this once

Four months, five broker spreadsheets, and dozens of self-sent links is not diligence.
Past a certain point, more research is a way of *not deciding* — it feels productive and
costs nothing socially. The cost is real though: it's the single largest recurring drain
on your personal attention, and it's competing with your kids' school budget and a
house build for the same mental slot.

Suggested close-out, one evening:

1. Write down your three constraints — monthly ceiling, must-haves, timing.
2. Take the best current number from each of the five sheets you already have.
3. Pick. Sign by **Aug 15**.
4. Then unsubscribe from every lease newsletter and archive `ME-READ`.

The specific car matters much less than closing the loop.

## How to use this file

- **Add** the moment you make a commitment to a person. Not later.
- **One owner, one date** per row. No owner means it's yours. No date means it's not real.
- **`WAITING` rows are the money.** Those are the things that die silently — a sub who
  never sent pricing, a client who never answered. Review them every single day.
- **Delete freely.** A stale row costs more than a missing one, because it teaches you
  to distrust the list. The instant you distrust it, you're back to remembering everything.
