## If you only do one thing

**Cut the pursuit list from 15+ to six.**

This is a *win rate* argument, not a workload one. Fifteen thin bids with rushed pricing
and generic decks lose to firms that resourced five properly. Hit rate is what the boss
and the project executive judge — and it's the only variable you fully control.

At 2 hours per pursuit per week that's 30+ hours before any delivery work, any
presentation, or any managing. The math has never closed. Six pursuits, properly
resourced, will win more work than fifteen rushed ones.

## The single rule that makes this work

> **Capture is free. Processing is scheduled.**

You will keep emailing yourself — that habit is good, don't fight it. What changes is
that once a week, for 30 minutes, the pile gets drained. Every item becomes exactly
one of four things:

1. **Do** — under 5 minutes, do it now
2. **Date** — it's a commitment, it gets a due date in Open Loops
3. **Decide** — it's an unresolved decision, it gets a *forcing date* in Open Loops
4. **Drop** — it was interesting, not important. Delete it. Most of the pile is this.

The fourth one is the one you're not doing. That's the whole problem.
