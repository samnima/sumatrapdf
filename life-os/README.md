**Goal (90 days): nothing falls through the cracks.**

Not "do more." Not "work faster." Just: every commitment you've made — to a client,
a sub, your boss, your team, your wife, your kids — is captured somewhere you trust,
with a date, and you see it without hunting.

## The one-paragraph diagnosis

You have a capture habit that works (emailing yourself) and **no processing step**.
So capture became a landfill: 624 self-sent threads, 267 never reopened. Meanwhile
your inbox stopped being a signal channel — it's ~50:1 promotional noise, so real
items are invisible by default. And your work life (RFPs, subs, clients, boss, team)
lives in an account this system can't see yet. Nothing here is a discipline problem.
It's a plumbing problem.

## The single rule that makes this work

> **Capture is free. Processing is scheduled.**

You will keep emailing yourself — that habit is good, don't fight it. What changes is
that once a week, for 30 minutes, the pile gets drained. Every item becomes exactly
one of four things:

1. **Do** — under 5 minutes, do it now
2. **Date** — it's a commitment, it gets a due date in Open Loops
3. **Decide** — it's an unresolved decision, it gets a *forcing date* in Open Loops
4. **Drop** — it was interesting, not important. Delete it. Most of the pile is this.

The fourth one is the one you're not doing. That's the whole problem.
