## B. Packages out to subs

This is where bids quietly die. A sub who says "yes, I'll price it" and then goes silent
costs you a whole pursuit, and you find out three days before it's due.

| Project | Trade | Sub / vendor | Sent | Due back | Confirmed? | Received? | Chased |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 193 Market St | | Everest (`eri@everest.al`) | | | | | |

Two rules that prevent almost every late-bid fire drill:

1. **"Confirmed?" is a separate column from "Sent."** Sending isn't coverage. A trade
   with one unconfirmed bidder is an uncovered trade, and you want to know that on day
   two, not day nine.
2. **Chase at 50% of elapsed time, not at the deadline.** Package due back in 10 days
   → you chase on day 5. Chasing on day 9 means you find out too late to find someone else.

## E. Project folders & binders

Your stated pain: going through project folders, linking with emails, seeing where
updates are needed.

**Standard structure per project** — same every time, so you never hunt:

```
<Project Name>/
  01-RFP-and-Addenda/
  02-Drawings-and-Specs/
  03-Sub-Bids/           ← one subfolder per trade
  04-Pricing-Binder/
  05-Correspondence/
  06-Submission/
  07-Presentation/
```

The reason folders drift out of sync with email is that filing is a *second* action
after reading. Two things that fix it without discipline:

- **Save attachments at the moment you read the mail**, not later. Later never comes.
- **Put the project name in every subject line** you control, so search does the
  linking for you and the folder is a backup, not the index.
